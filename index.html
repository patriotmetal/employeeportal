FAQ & Resources

Expand any item. You can edit these later or move them to a shared Google Doc / Notion.

What is included in a standard concrete slab quote? ▼
Typically: 4″ slab, vapor barrier, 6-gauge wire mesh (center), and 12″×12″ perimeter footing. Dirt work, extra leveling beyond 1–2 free loads, and pump truck / grooving are usually extra. Always confirm with the current pricing sheet.
When do we use 6″ slab vs 4″? ▼
6″ is common for heavier equipment, higher point loads, or when the engineer / local code requires it. The calculator applies the 6″ markup automatically when selected.
How do spray foam thicknesses work? ▼
We currently price 1″ and 2″. The calculator uses customer rates of $1.85 / $2.75 per sq ft respectively, plus a standard $400 travel/rental allowance. Contractor (our cost) rates are lower and shown for margin reference.
Where can I find wind / snow load requirements? ▼
Use the Zipcode Tool for location data and a starting point. Final design loads should always be verified with the project engineer or the free ATC Hazards tool (hazards.atcouncil.org) and local building department. Do not rely solely on regional averages for stamped drawings.
Who do I contact for pricing updates? ▼
Update the concrete and foam pricing tables in this portal (or the source spreadsheets) whenever vendor rates change. Keep a dated note of the last update for the team.

Zipcode Locator & Load Guidance

Enter a 5-digit US ZIP code to get location details, a Google Maps link, and whether the area has Texas Windstorm Certificate requirements.

Windstorm Requirement = YES when the county is in a designated high-wind / catastrophe area that commonly requires special certification or elevated wind design for insurance and permits:
• Texas: the 14 first-tier coastal counties + parts of Harris County east of Hwy 146 (WPI-8 / TWIA)
• Florida: Entire state (elevated wind design requirements statewide)
• LA, MS, AL, GA, SC, NC: primary Gulf & Atlantic coastal counties with elevated hurricane/wind requirements
County is determined from coordinates for accuracy. Always verify the exact property with the local building department.

Windstorm / WPI-8 Checklist (Texas coastal)

  • Confirm property is in a first-tier coastal county (or Harris east of Hwy 146) using the Zipcode Tool
  • Building must be designed/inspected to applicable windstorm building code
  • Certificate of Compliance (WPI-8 / WPI-8-E) required for TWIA eligibility on new work / major improvements
  • TDI Windstorm Inspection Program: tdi.texas.gov/wind · (800) 248-6032
  • Common items needing certification: new structure, additions, roof replacement, exterior doors/windows, major envelope work
  • Do not promise TWIA coverage — customer’s agent handles eligibility and certification paperwork
  • Flag coastal jobs early so engineering and inspection sequencing are correct
